    How PosturePal works

    Posture. A pose model finds your nose, ears, shoulders and hips. PosturePal measures neck length (slouch), shoulder angle, head roll, torso lean and head drift — all relative to shoulder width, so distance from the camera doesn't matter. Calibrate once while sitting tall to set your personal baseline.

    Fatigue. A face model outputs blink and jaw-open blendshapes. Short closures (60–500 ms) count as blinks; long closures raise PERCLOS (percent eyes closed); a wide-open jaw for 0.8 s counts as a yawn. These feed a 0–100 fatigue index.

    Coaching. Bad posture held past your alert threshold triggers a gentle chime. Break reminders follow 20-20-20. Everything runs locally — no video is stored or sent anywhere.

    Limits. Front-facing seated pose only; side profiles and standing desks degrade accuracy. Fatigue is a heuristic wellness hint, not a medical measurement.
